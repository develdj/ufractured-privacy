µFractured — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Italiano

µFractured is a single-player psychological adventure with optional multiplayer features. It is built to work without accounts and without any server operated by the developer. This page explains what that means for your data.

The short version

  • The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • Some optional features rely on Apple services, which are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

Save slots, settings, statistics and in-game progress are kept locally on your iPhone or iPad. They never leave the device through the app. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits scores to leaderboards and reports achievements, and you can invite friends to play. Game Center is operated by Apple: your alias, scores and achievements are handled by Apple under its own privacy terms. The developer only sees the public leaderboards that every player sees.

Local multiplayer (optional)

Nearby play uses Bluetooth and the local network (Apple's MultipeerConnectivity) to connect directly to other devices close to you. Messages, chat and game moves travel directly between the devices and are not sent to any server. The app asks for permission before using Bluetooth or the local network.

Translation and on-device AI

Multiplayer chat can be translated, and some characters can answer in free text. Both features use Apple's on-device frameworks (Translation and Apple Intelligence Foundation Models). Text is processed on your device; when these features are unavailable, the game uses fixed text instead.

Voice input (optional)

If you use voice input, the app asks for microphone and speech recognition permission. Speech is transcribed by Apple's Speech framework, which may send audio to Apple to perform the recognition, as described in Apple's privacy terms. The developer never receives or stores audio or transcripts.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details; the app only learns which items you own so it can unlock them.

Notifications (optional)

If you allow them, the app schedules local reminders on your device. They are created on the device itself; no push server is involved.

Children

The game deals with mature themes, including trauma, and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
